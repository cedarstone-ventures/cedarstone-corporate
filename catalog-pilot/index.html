Know what belongs in the pilot.

The proposed $79 scope covers one consistent table layout. We agree the columns, required fields and selected pages before accepting a job.

Included in the proposed scope

  • Up to 100 product records from up to 10 pages.
  • Up to 8 source fields, plus page references.
  • A CSV and a readable exception record.
  • Checks against the agreed source and columns.

Outside this pilot

  • Private files, logins or customer records.
  • Images, new descriptions or variant expansion.
  • Live stock, retail pricing or product advice.
  • Direct store upload or a universal import template.

A public link alone does not establish permission to reuse a catalog. Source rights, privacy and readability still need review.

    Before you plan a job

    Will the file import directly into Shopify?

    The pilot produces agreed columns. Shopify and other platforms may need images, variants, handles or other fields that this scope excludes. Confirm the destination schema before accepting a job.

    What happens when a required value is missing?

    A required gap must be resolved from an eligible source or the job is declined before payment. Optional blanks are agreed and listed in the exception record. We do not invent product facts.

    Is every public supplier PDF eligible?

    Eligibility depends on source rights, privacy, legibility and the selected layout. A public URL or a successful scope check does not by itself approve a document for processing.

    Can I send a private catalog or store login?

    This pilot is for eligible public supplier catalogs. Keep private files, customer details and account credentials out of the request.

    When can I order?

    The pilot is being prepared. Enquiries and payments are not open yet. The published opening will include the request route, final terms and any applicable taxes before payment.
