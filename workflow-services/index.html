Company information

Do we need to replace our existing software?

We first check whether your current software can solve the problem through settings or an included feature. If it can, that may be the entire job. A custom build is considered when the agreed need requires it.

Can you work with sensitive client information?

Start with a description or a redacted example. Do not send passwords, financial account details or client records in the initial enquiry. Client records stay out of AI models. We agree access, confidentiality, local processing and retention before any real files are shared. Some regulated or high-risk work may be outside our scope.

Is a call required?

An initial enquiry can be handled by email. If it will make the scope clearer, we can arrange a short call. The final scope and price are confirmed in writing.

What happens if the scope changes?

We explain the change and agree any additional price or timing before proceeding. Ongoing monitoring, extra integrations and new features are separate work.
