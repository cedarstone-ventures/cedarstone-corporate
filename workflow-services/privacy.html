Privacy, plainly.

Updated 12 November 2026. This page explains the information used by this service website. Cedarstone Ventures LLC can be contacted at info@cedarstonellc.com.

Browsing this website

The website contains a fictional demonstration. Its enquiry link opens your email application; it does not submit a form in the background. The page code does not include advertising pixels or visitor analytics. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to operate the site.

Contacting us

If you email us, your email provider and ours process that message. We use the information you choose to send to respond to your enquiry and, if appropriate, discuss a project. Please begin with a description or redacted example rather than client records, credentials or sensitive information.

Project information

Client records are processed with reviewed local scripts and are not sent to AI models. Before project files are shared, we agree the purpose, access method, confidentiality and retention arrangements in writing. Website demonstrations are not permission to upload or disclose another person’s information.

Questions

Contact the address above with a privacy question or a request concerning information you have provided. The separate company website privacy page covers the existing corporate website.
