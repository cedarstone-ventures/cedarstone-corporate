The scope check

Answers are processed in your browser memory. They are not sent to Cedarstone, an AI provider or an analytics service. The page does not ask for a name, email, source link, file or payment details.

The downloadable summary is created in your browser and saved only if you choose to download it. Clearing or reloading the form discards its answers. The page does not use cookies or browser storage for the check.

Page access

The preview server receives normal page and asset requests. This preview uses no analytics, advertising pixels or third-party fonts. The live service will need its own accurate privacy notice before enquiries open.

Company website

The existing company website has a separate privacy notice. This preview does not change how that website or its email contact works.
