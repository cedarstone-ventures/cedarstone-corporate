Privacy.

Updated 12 November 2026. This page covers cedarstonellc.com, including its workflow and data service pages.

Browsing the website

The site has no account registration, client upload or background contact-form submission. Its code does not include advertising pixels, visitor analytics or tracking cookies. The site uses system fonts, with page resources served from this website.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information under its privacy statement.

Enquiries and project information

The contact links open your email application. If you email us, your email provider and ours process the message. Cedarstone uses the information you choose to provide to respond, discuss a project and keep necessary business records.

Begin with a description or redacted example. Do not send passwords, financial account details or client records in an initial enquiry. Before project data is shared, we agree the purpose, access method, confidentiality and retention arrangements in writing.

Client records are processed with reviewed local scripts and are not sent to AI models. The public example uses fictional records. We do not sell personal information.

Questions and requests

Contact info@cedarstonellc.com with questions or requests concerning information you have provided. We consider requests in light of applicable requirements and any records we must retain.
