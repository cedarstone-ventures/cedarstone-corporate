Privacy

What we collect, which is almost nothing.

Last updated 25 July 2026

This policy covers cedarstonellc.com and the brand sites Cedarstone Ventures LLC operates. We have written it in plain language because a privacy policy nobody can read is not a disclosure.

We do not ask you for anything

This website has no account, no login, no newsletter signup and no contact form. There is nothing here for you to fill in, so there is nothing for us to store. If you email us, we have your email address because you sent it to us, and we use it only to reply.

What our host records automatically

Our sites are served by a static hosting provider. Like every web server, it records standard technical request logs, which can include your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser type. We do not have a dashboard that lets us look you up, and we do not combine these logs with anything else.

Fonts

We load typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives the request, including your IP address, under its own privacy terms. We do not send Google anything about you beyond that request.

What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising or advertising trackers.
  • We do not run behavioural analytics or session recording.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information. There is nothing collected here to sell.
  • We do not set cookies for tracking.

Buying from us

Our products are sold through marketplaces such as Amazon. When you buy there, that marketplace is the merchant and its privacy policy governs the transaction. We do not receive your payment details, and in most cases we never learn your name or address at all.

Children

Our products are for adults managing property and taxes. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information a company holds about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Ask us and we will answer honestly, including when the honest answer is that we hold nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. If a change is material, it will be described here rather than quietly rewritten.
